## Welcome!

I am Niccolò Zaccaria, a PhD candidate in Economics at [Tilburg University](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/).

My research focuses on price rigidities and firm dynamics. I also study how AI and large language models affect economic signals and aggregate outcomes.

My supervisors are [Burak Uras](https://sites.google.com/site/burakruras/home) and [Sigrid Suetens](https://sigridsuetens.weebly.com/).

In Spring 2026 I visited the Department of Economics at New York University.
